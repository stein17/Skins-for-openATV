PLi FullNight FHD - Skinpart previews
====================================

The setup automatically looks for a preview whose basename is identical to the
selected skinpart XML. PNG, JPG and JPEG are accepted. The preview folder
mirrors the allScreens folder.

Examples:
  allScreens/weather/weather_Kompakt_oben_links.xml
  preview/weather/weather_Kompakt_oben_links.png

  allScreens/vpnip/VPN_Infobar_left_bottom.xml
  preview/vpnip/VPN_Infobar_left_bottom.jpg

  allScreens/weather_animated/weather_Icons_Animiert.xml
  preview/weather_animated/weather_Icons_Animiert.png

Recommended image ratio for the setup field: 374 x 210 (16:9).
If no matching image exists, the preview field simply stays hidden.
